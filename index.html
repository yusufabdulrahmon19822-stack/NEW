FAQ

Questions before we start?

Do you offer revisions?

Yes. We agree on the revision process for your project, and you can review the work and request necessary changes before final delivery.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the type and complexity of the project. I'll give you an estimated delivery time before we start.

Can I request only one service?

Yes. You can request a landing page, graphic design, AI UGC advert, or a combination of services.

Do I need everything ready before contacting you?

No. If you have an idea but aren't sure how to structure it, we can discuss what you need.

Can you design for an existing business?

Yes. The work can be created for an existing brand, product, service or new business.
